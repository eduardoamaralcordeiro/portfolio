About me

A little about me.

Self-taught digital creator with more than 10 years of hands-on computer work, producing finished creative work across the areas AI creative systems are now judged on.

Enterprise communication (slide decks and academic presentations), marketing and advertising (social content for medical congresses, sales funnels and brand identity), staging and layouts (event posters, mascot and visual identity), interactive digital products (a native app and responsive websites), and short-form video for social media.

I'm ambitious about big things, and I give everything I take on my full commitment. When I start something, I see it through: I put in the hours, sweat the details and don't settle for good enough. Medical school shapes the way I work. It taught me critical thinking, so I question assumptions and weigh the evidence before I decide. It taught me discipline, so I keep learning under pressure. And it showed me the pace an operating room demands, where every move has to be fast, precise and calm. I bring that same mix of rigor and speed to every brief, every layout and every piece of feedback.

As a medical student trained in close observation and critical appraisal of evidence, I judge work against explicit criteria and explain my reasoning in writing.
