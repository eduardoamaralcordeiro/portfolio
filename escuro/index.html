A little about me.

Self-taught digital creator with more than 10 years of hands-on computer work. I make finished work in the same areas where AI creative output is now judged.

Enterprise communication (slide decks and academic presentations), marketing and advertising (social content for medical congresses, sales funnels and brand identity), staging and layouts (event posters, mascot and visual identity), interactive digital products (a native app and responsive websites), and short-form video for social media.

When I start something, I finish it, details included. Medical school shapes the way I work: I question assumptions and weigh the evidence before I decide, I keep learning under pressure, and the operating room taught me to move fast without losing precision.

As a medical student trained in close observation and critical appraisal of evidence, I judge work against explicit criteria and explain my reasoning in writing.
